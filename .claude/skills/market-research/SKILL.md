# Market Research

Targeted research to inform Forge Fire's income-generation projects
(Growth Rings, WhisperBOT, SecondMe) — not general AI news-watching. Replaces
the old "weekly market research → Telegram" idea as an on-demand routine.

## Steps

1. **Anchor to a real question**
   - Before searching, pick a specific angle tied to an active project, e.g.:
     - "What are people paying for in AI transcription/note tools?" (WhisperBOT)
     - "What's the competitive landscape for 'digital twin' / personal AI
       agent products?" (SecondMe)
     - "What pricing models work for small AI agent SaaS tools?"
   - If Boss didn't specify, pick the angle most relevant to whatever Phase 2B
     item is currently active in `plans/roadmap.md`

2. **Research** (WebSearch)
   - 3-5 targeted searches max — this is meant to be lightweight, not a full
     `deep-research` run. If Boss wants a deep multi-source report, use the
     `deep-research` skill instead.
   - Prioritize recent sources (last 3-6 months) given how fast this space moves

3. **Synthesize**
   - What's the finding, and what does it suggest for the relevant Forge Fire
     project specifically? Avoid generic "AI is growing fast" summaries.
   - Note any concrete opportunities (a gap, a pricing benchmark, a tool to
     build on) worth adding to `plans/roadmap.md` Phase 2B

## Output format

2-4 bullet findings + 1 "so what for Forge Fire" line each. If something is
actionable, suggest it as a roadmap addition rather than just FYI.

## Guardrails

- No spending money based on research findings without Boss's go-ahead.
- Keep it short — this should feel like a quick scouting report, not a
  consulting deliverable.
